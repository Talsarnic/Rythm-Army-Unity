## Full roster
Each of the 10 classes receives body/evolution art, equipment layers, all required states, portrait/card/icon art, and class-specific hero presentation.

## Quality gates
1. Readable at gameplay scale.
2. Equipment changes silhouette or major material/color blocks.
3. Animation remains readable with equipment equipped.
4. Palette and lighting match the approved style.
5. Asset works in the actual Unity scene.
6. No placeholder/procedural replacement is considered final art.
